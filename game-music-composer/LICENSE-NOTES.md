## Studio Chamber recordings

The Chamber palette and its compact projection include adapted VSCO 2 Community Edition recordings by Versilian Studios (Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote), released under CC0-1.0. Source: https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/ ; pinned upstream revision 440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e. Per-recording source URLs and hashes are in data/soundbank-provenance.json. Velvet and Circuit synthesis is original project code. Recorded sources are not claimed as project-authored recordings.

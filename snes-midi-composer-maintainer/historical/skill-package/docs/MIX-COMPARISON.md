# Mix comparison — v2.1 to v2.2

## Problems addressed

- generic low-piano-like bass identity;
- horns, brass and synth leads dominating from source amplitude;
- drums buried beneath harmony and ensemble layers;
- event gain doing too many jobs at once;
- limited fast-tempo ornamentation;
- no browser controls for monitor level, output ceiling or tempo scale.

## v2.2 changes

### Sample palette

The bank grows from 38 to 46 samples and adds:

- upright bass v2;
- electric finger bass;
- picked bass;
- analog bass;
- sub bass;
- bowed contrabass;
- punch kick;
- body snare.

### Mix engine

- seven functional buses;
- calibrated source trims;
- category-aware bass routing;
- lower horn/synth/pulse/bell trims;
- stronger drum targets;
- parallel drum body and transient layers;
- bass reinforcement and mono discipline;
- lead-priority automation;
- master ceiling near −1 dBFS.

### Measured result

- 100 OGG and 100 MP3 renders;
- loudness range: −17.06 to −14.77 LUFS;
- mean loudness: −16.11 LUFS;
- loudness standard deviation: 0.78 LU;
- maximum sample peak: −1.03 dBFS;
- exact loop durations.

### MIDI

- 31,546 note-on events;
- velocity range 31–127;
- CC7 track balance separated from velocity;
- CC11 phrase and section expression;
- CC10 pan and CC91 spatial send.

### Workstation

- master monitor slider;
- vertical ceiling slider beside the output meter;
- L/R meter with peak hold;
- centered tempo control from 60 to 150 percent;
- expressive piano roll driven by velocity;
- deterministic generation-harness editor.
